Why Meridian

Designed around your career, not against it.

Every element of the eMBA — the schedule, the case studies, the cohort — is built for senior professionals who lead teams today and aim to lead organisations tomorrow.

Zero career interruption

Classes run on alternate weekends with live-online midweek sessions. You apply Monday-morning what you learned Saturday — in your own company.

ASEAN-first curriculum

Case studies on Malaysian GLCs, Islamic finance, regional supply chains and digital economies — not recycled Western textbooks.

A cohort worth the fee

Learn alongside directors, founders and senior managers averaging 12 years of experience. Your classmates become your board of advisors.

Practitioner faculty

Taught by professors who consult for Fortune 500 and Bursa-listed firms, joined by C-suite guest speakers every module.

Global immersion week

A one-week residency with a partner school abroad — recent cohorts visited Singapore, Shanghai and London.

Executive coaching included

Six one-on-one sessions with an ICF-certified leadership coach, plus a 360° assessment at entry and exit.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need a GMAT or GRE score?

No. Candidates with 8+ years of professional experience are exempt. Those with 5–8 years may be asked to complete our internal admissions assessment instead.

Can I manage this alongside a demanding job?

The programme is designed exactly for that. You're on campus two weekends a month; online sessions are recorded; and assignments are built around applying concepts to your own organisation, so study time doubles as work time.

Is the programme accredited?

The eMBA is accredited by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA) and recognised for HRD Corp claims by eligible employers.

Can my employer sponsor me?

Yes — roughly half our participants are partially or fully employer-sponsored. Our admissions team provides a sponsorship proposal template and ROI briefing you can present to your HR or leadership team.

What if I miss a weekend due to work travel?

All sessions are recorded, and each module offers a make-up pathway. Many of our participants travel regionally for work; the programme is built to absorb that.

When is the application deadline?

Applications for the January 2027 intake close on 30 November 2026. Seats are offered on a rolling basis, and the early-bird rebate applies before 31 October 2026.
