Research date: 3 September 2026. Written in English with BM examples. All quotes are verbatim, 15 words or fewer, and come only from pages actually opened (Section 1). Programme facts are taken from `src/lib/content.ts` and `SEO-CONTENT-GOVERNANCE.md`; nothing in this guide changes a fact.

Access notes: `bharian.com.my`, `hmetro.com.my`, `utusan.com.my` returned HTTP 403 to the fetcher (including AMP URLs) and `astroawani.com` blocks the crawler at domain level. Those pages were read through the Browser pane instead. Astro Awani was only read at section-index level (headlines and ledes). `ukm.my` programme pages returned 404 or an unreadable PDF; `oum.edu.my` programme pages returned 403; Siti Khadijah collection pages expose only English navigation, so its blog posts were used for BM prose. Siti Khadijah Facebook/Instagram copy was not fetched and is not quoted.

## 2. Register findings by group (with evidence)

### 2.1 Mainstream news (BH, Utusan, Sinar Harian, Awani)

**Headline structure.** Subject + bare verb (the meN- prefix is dropped in headlines only), no articles, numerals not words, attribution after a dash.
- "MARA, MISC meterai MoU, taja 50 pelajar MRSM ceburi bidang maritim" (1.1)
- "Kumpulan BPMB rekod pengambilan kohort biasiswa BAGUS terbesar" (1.9) — "rekod" used as a verb.
- "Semua 18 pelajar terbaik STPM peringkat kebangsaan ditawar biasiswa ke universiti awam" (1.7) — headline passive without "-kan".
- "Perkhidmatan awam perlu berbelanja ikut peruntukan – KSN" (1.10) — Awani's dash-attribution pattern.
- Feature headlines are aphoristic: "Tiada duit bukan penghalang sambung belajar" (1.8); "Biasiswa MSU perkasa akses pendidikan" (1.3).

**Lede and dateline.** Hard news opens with a CAPITALISED dateline and colon; features capitalise the first word.
- "PETALING JAYA: Bank Pembangunan Malaysia Berhad (BPMB) menganugerahkan biasiswa kepada 18 pelajar" (1.9)
- "BAGI kebanyakan anak muda, surat tawaran ke universiti adalah tiket untuk mengubah masa depan." (1.8)

**Sentence length and rhythm.** 30–45-word sentences chained with "bagi", "menerusi", "sekali gus", "selain", "termasuk"; one sentence per paragraph is normal.
- "sekali gus mencatatkan pengambilan kohort penerima tahunan terbesar sejak program itu diperkenalkan pada 2013" (1.9)
- "Program berkenaan merekodkan jumlah pelaburan terkumpul melebihi RM17 juta" (1.9)

**Passive "di-" vs active.** Institutions act in the passive; named people act in the active with a speech verb. Passives seen: "ditawarkan", "diperkenalkan", "disalurkan", "diperluaskan", "dilaksanakan", "diiktiraf", "didedahkan".
- "Menteri Pendidikan, Fadhlina Sidek, berkata inisiatif julung kali diperkenalkan bagi kohort STPM 2025" (1.7)
- "pelajar juga menerima tiga sijil yang diiktiraf industri" (1.2)

**Attribution phrases.** "katanya", "Beliau berkata", "Menurutnya", "Ujarnya", "Tegasnya", "Tambahnya", "katanya dalam kenyataan" (1.7, 1.8, 1.9).
- "Tambahnya, program itu menyediakan bantuan yuran pengajian penuh kepada pelajar daripada keluarga B40 yang layak." (1.8)

**Address forms.** Hard news never addresses the reader; subjects are "pelajar", "mereka", "penerima". Only the advisory desk (Sinar Bestari) uses "anda", and only at the close or in a CTA:
- "Semoga senarai ini dapat membantu anda membuat keputusan terbaik." (1.6)
- "Rebut peluang, mohon seawal mungkin dan terus berusaha." (1.6)
- "Berita Telus & Tulus hanya untuk anda setiap hari. Langgan Sekarang!" (1.3 subscription box)

**Formality markers and spelling.** "program" (never "programme"), "kohort", "prasiswazah", "ijazah sarjana muda", "yuran pengajian", "biasiswa", "tajaan/penajaan", "pengambilan", "diiktiraf/pengiktirafan", "kelayakan", "kepimpinan", "sekali gus" (two words), "menerusi", "berkenaan", "tersebut", "julung kali".
- "Kriteria kelayakan berbeza mengikut biasiswa namun kebiasaannya melibatkan beberapa perkara" (1.6)
- "memastikan kelayakan memenuhi piawaian yang ditetapkan oleh Agensi Kelayakan Malaysia (MQA) adalah penting" (1.5)

**Numerals, currency, dates.** No space after RM; thousands comma; "juta/bilion" spelled; "peratus" spelled in prose; year alone or "pada 2013"; prose dates "20 Ogos 2026" / "16 Jun 2026". Bylines on Media Prima sites use an English-order stamp ("Jun 18, 2026 @ 3:01pm") — a CMS artefact, not prose style. Sinar Bestari's timestamp even uses "23 Dec 2025" — also a CMS artefact; body text always uses BM month names.
- "Selain biasiswa, masing-masing turut menerima wang tunai bernilai RM1,000, plak dan sijil" (1.7)
- "Projek lulus RM36.6 bilion tapi peruntukan MoF hanya RM12 bilion - Setiausaha Akhbar KKDW" (1.10)

**Loanwords and programme names.** English programme/award names are kept verbatim, roman (no italics), with a BM or English acronym in brackets on first mention; institutions are not translated.
- "Anugerah Biasiswa Prasiswazah Kumpulan BPMB (BAGUS) 2026" (1.9)
- "TVET Centre of Excellence Award (Automotive Technical Education)" (1.2)
- English jargon words get single quotes: 'smart board', sistem "patching" (1.7, 2.6).

**Punctuation.** Colons only after datelines; dashes for attribution; almost no exclamation marks; sentence-case headlines; commas around appositive titles ("Menteri Pendidikan, Fadhlina Sidek, berkata").

### 2.2 Tabloid / popular (Kosmo, Harian Metro)

**Headline structure.** Shorter, a number or a quoted phrase as the hook, an ampersand is acceptable, emotive verbs ("pacu", "sasar", "sedia").
- "10 'talian hayat' sambung belajar" (2.1)
- "12A+ pacu impian anak Terengganu, sasar Imperial College menerusi Biasiswa Yayasan UEM" (2.2)
- "TVET laluan pendidikan fleksibel & progresif" (2.7)

**Lede devices.** Proverb, scene-setting, or a colloquial direct quote.
- "KATA pepatah, asalkan ada kemahuan pasti ada jalan." (2.1)
- "Saya bersyukur sangat ada orang nak percaya dengan saya" (2.4) — "nak" kept in speech.
- "DALAM dunia pekerjaan yang semakin kompetitif, bidang kesetiausahaan bukan lagi sekadar menguruskan fail" (2.3)

**Rhythm.** Very short one-line paragraphs alternate with long ones: "Namun, persepsi itu kini berubah dengan ketara." (2.7). Body sentences in hard-news tabloid pieces are otherwise identical in grammar to BH (compare 1.7 and 2.6, the same event).

**Emotive lexicon.** "sebak", "teruja", "rezeki terindah" (2.4); "impian", "kisah kegigihan", "talian hayat", "cerah", "gemilang".

**Address and colloquialisms.** Still third person in narration; colloquial forms appear inside quotes or in soft connectives: "buat" for "untuk" ("panduan buat pelajar-pelajar", 2.1); ministerial quote "Kalau tahun ini satu, tahun depan kita minta lima kalau boleh," (2.6). Op-eds use "pelajar perlu…" imperatives to a third-person reader (2.8).

**Numbers and dates.** Same conventions as mainstream: "Jumlah pinjaman maksimum ditawarkan biasanya hingga RM6,800 bagi asasi, RM21,000 bagi diploma" (2.1); "Tarikh permohonan Fasa Pertama dari 29 Februari hingga 29 Mac 2024" (2.8); "RM7.9 bilion", "98 peratus" (2.7).

**English inside BM.** Single-quoted English phrases: 'Work-Based Learning', 'Industry on Campus', 'high-growth', 'high-value' (2.7). Abbreviated CTAs like "Chat Dgn Kami" (4.7) belong to this register, not to formal marketing.

**What makes it feel tabloid.** Number-hook headlines, proverb ledes, human-story framing, reported speech with "nak/tak", emotive nouns, ampersands, imperatives ("Cepat mohon", 1.6 shares this), and one-sentence paragraphs.

### 2.3 Government and regulator (HRD Corp, MQA, KPT, MyGov, DBP)

**Headings and structure.** ALL-CAPS section labels and nominalised headings: "MAJIKAN", "PENYEDIA LATIHAN", "INDIVIDU", "SKIM BANTUAN LATIHAN (SBL)", "GARIS PANDUAN", "LOG MASUK", "KETAHUI LEBIH LANJUT" (3.1–3.3). MQA FAQ pages are Q&A pairs in full sentences.

**Address.** HRD Corp addresses the employer or individual as "anda" in menus and benefit copy; regulatory text uses roles ("majikan", "penyedia latihan", "PPT", "pemohon", "calon").
- "Jadilah majikan berdaftar di HRD Corp" (3.1)
- "Nilai keberkesanan program latihan anda dengan Penilaian HRD Corp" (3.1)
- "memberikan anda peluang untuk melatih semula serta meningkatkan kemahiran" (3.6)

**Official HRD Corp terminology (BM pages).**
- Grant application: "Permohonan Geran"; guidelines list "Rayuan Geran", "Penarikan Balik Geran", "Pengubahsuaian Geran" (3.2).
- Claim: "Garis Panduan Tuntutan"; "Panduan untuk mengemukakan tuntutan"; "Prosedur Rayuan Tuntutan Geran Levi Latihan" (3.2).
- Allowable Cost Matrix: the BM page labels it "Kos Matriks yang dibenarkan" (3.2) — a calque; the English name is still the identifier used everywhere else (3.7, 3.8).
- HRD Corp claimable: the product name stays in English even on BM pages — "HRD Corp Claimable Course" (3.2, 3.4, 3.5). Descriptive BM: "Kos latihan ini boleh dituntut oleh majikan setelah tamat latihan." (3.4)
- Who applies and when: "haruslah dihantar oleh majikan sebelum tarikh latihan bermula" (3.3); English FAQ: "Application must be submitted by employers before training date commencement" (3.8).
- Deadlines: "program latihan mesti dimulakan dalam tempoh enam (6) bulan dari tarikh permohonan geran latihan dibuat" (3.3); "Training claims must be submitted within six (6) months from the completion date" (3.8).
- Provider side: "Penyedia latihan boleh menuntut Bayaran Latihan hanya selepas latihan dijalankan." (3.5); "Penyedia Latihan Berdaftar HRD Corp", "Majikan Berdaftar HRD Corp" (capitalised roles, 3.5).
- Documents: "Dokumen yang Diperlukan: Sebutharga / Invois, Jadual Latihan / Kandungan Kursus" (3.3); "Profil Pelatih".
- Levy: "Faedah Sumbangan Levi Oleh Majikan", "Pembayaran levi" (3.1, 3.4); system name "e-TRiS" (3.2) — note HRD Corp itself is inconsistent ("e-TRIS" on 3.3).

**Official MQA terminology.**
- "Daftar Kelayakan Malaysia (Malaysian Qualifications Register, MQR) adalah titik rujukan untuk program yang diiktiraf" (3.9) — English name in italics inside brackets, then acronym.
- Full accreditation: "satu perakuan rasmi kepada PPT bahawa sijil, diploma atau ijazah yang akan dianugerahkan" (3.9); "Akreditasi Sementara" / "Akreditasi Penuh"; verb form "diakredit": "bersifat berterusan (perpetual) bermula daripada tarikh program tersebut diakredit" (3.12).
- Recognition: "pengesahan kewujudan, kesahan atau kesahihan sesuatu program dan kelayakan" (3.9); "Pengiktirafan Kelayakan" (3.11).
- Professional bodies: "Semua permohonan program badan profesional perlu dikemukakan kepada MQA untuk mendapatkan akreditasi" (3.10).
- MQA uses "fi" for fee in regulatory context ("Berapakah jumlah fi bagi permohonan program Akreditasi Sementara dan Akreditasi Penuh", 3.10) and "caj" for a charge ("Caj yang dikenakan bagi pengambilan sijil Perakuan Akreditasi ialah RM 100.00.", 3.13). Universities use "yuran". For the site, "yuran" is correct; "fi" is regulator jargon.
- Levels: "Tahap 3 hingga Tahap 8", "Ijazah Sarjana Muda (Tahap 6, MQF)" (3.9, 4.5). "Kerangka Kelayakan Malaysia (MQF)".
- MQA does not confirm individuals: "Pengesahan kelayakan individu bagi program dalam dan luar negara tidak akan dilaksanakan oleh MQA." (3.11)

**KPT and MyGov.**
- "Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi (KPT) komited memastikan akses pendidikan tinggi yang inklusif, adil dan berkualiti" (3.14)
- "Semua permohonan akan diproses berdasarkan prinsip meritokrasi, syarat kelayakan akademik serta tertakluk kepada ketetapan" (3.14) — "tertakluk kepada" is the official conditional.
- "boleh dipertimbangkan untuk mengikuti program-program tertentu" (3.14) — the natural BM for "may be considered for".
- "Pelajar lepasan SPM yang layak dan memenuhi syarat berpeluang untuk mendapatkan penajaan" (3.15); "Tarikh dikemaskini: 10/12/2025" (3.15). Date in statement header: "14 MEI 2026" (3.14).

**Numbers.** Official style writes the word and the numeral: "enam (6) bulan", "dua kali (2X) setahun" (4.3), "tiga (3) kredit" (4.1). Percent sign appears in tables ("3%", 3.4; "60% / 40%", 4.8) but "peratus" in prose.

**DBP checks (3.17).**
- eksekutif: "(orang atau golongan) yg mempunyai kuasa dan tanggungjawab berkaitan dgn pentadbiran atau pengurusan" — standard.
- kepimpinan: "keupayaan memimpin: ~ melalui teladan" — standard; "kepemimpinan" is not the headword.
- pensijilan: "pemberian atau penganugerahan sijil setelah seseorang atau sesuatu mencapai atau memenuhi syarat-syarat" — standard.
- pengiktirafan: "perihal mengiktiraf (status, kedudukan, dsb sesuatu), pengakuan" — standard.
- kohort: "Carian kata tiada di dalam kamus terkini." but present in Istilah (70 entries) — acceptable technical term, as news usage confirms (1.7, 1.9).
- e-mel: headword with hyphen — use "e-mel", not "emel" or "email".
- dalam talian: headword shown; "atas talian" is not the DBP form (UUM and AeU still use "atas talian" — treat as informal variant).

**What makes it feel official.** Caps labels, nominalisations ("permohonan", "penghantaran", "pelaksanaan"), "tertakluk kepada", "hendaklah/haruslah/mesti", "dikemukakan", "berkenaan/tersebut", bracketed acronyms, word+numeral, and no second person outside menus.

### 2.4 University and executive-education marketing in BM

**Headline and heading habits.** Sentence-case or Title Case with "anda"; caps for badge lines; questions as openers in online-programme copy.
- "Maklumat Penting Yang Anda Perlu Tahu Mengenai Diploma Eksekutif UM" (4.1)
- "KENAPA PILIH UM" (4.1) — colloquial "kenapa" even at UM; "Mengapa Kursus ini" (4.5, 4.6) is the more formal variant.
- "Adakah anda ingin mengambil kursus Ijazah Sarjana Pentadbiran Perniagaan (MBA) dalam talian?" (4.5)

**Working-adult positioning.** Universities say "pengalaman bekerja", "separuh masa", "profesional yang bekerja", "golongan pengurus dan eksekutif"; none says "pengurus bekerja".
- "Mempunyai sekurang-kurangnya enam (6) bulan pengalaman bekerja" (4.1)
- "untuk golongan pengurus dan eksekutif" (4.8)
- "Kemungkinannya, anda merancang masa depan anda sambil mengimbangi tanggungjawab kerja dan keluarga." (4.5)
- Warning example of a calque to avoid: "tanpa berputus asa dengan pekerjaan semasa dan masa keluarga mereka" (4.5) — "without giving up" mistranslated as "without despairing".

**Fees.** "Jumlah Yuran" / "jumlah yuran pengajian" with two decimals in formal tables; both "RM19,563.00" (4.2) and "RM 19,450.00" (4.5), "RM 1,938.00" (4.3) occur. Registration fee is separated: "Yuran Pendaftaran RM300.00 (Dibayar Sekali Sahaja)" (4.2). **Rhythm.** A long descriptive sentence (20–25 words) followed by a short one (5–8 words): "Cuti sekolah tak lama lagi." (5.4). Paragraphs of two to three sentences; fragment subheadings ("Fabrik sejuk dan tidak panas", "Labuh dan saiz").

**Colloquial and code-mixed forms.** "macam", "tapi", "nak", "tak", "pun", "-lah" (ibulah, Belilah, pilihlah); English words "check list", "online", "travel", "on-the-go", "secure", "design" — "Inilah telekung travel yang memang dicipta untuk wanita on-the-go." (5.4). Exclamation marks: none in the three posts; warmth comes from pronouns, connectives and cadence, not punctuation. **What to borrow for this site.** The "anda"-centred sentence, the worry-then-reassurance pattern, "Sebab itu/Jadi" connectives, the short follow-up sentence, and the closing "Semoga…". What not to borrow: "nak/tak/macam", "-lah", English code-mixing, and "jatuh cinta"-type emotive promises.

## 5. Do / Don't

**Do**
1. Use "anda" as the only second-person pronoun; keep it out of fee, HRD Corp and credential fact sentences.
2. Keep official identifiers in English, roman type, no quotes: HRD Corp Claimable Course, Allowable Cost Matrix, e-TRiS, CMI Certificate of Recognition, Chartered Manager, Executive MBA on Future Ready Business Leadership.
3. Gloss an English official term once per page in brackets, MQA-style: "Daftar Kelayakan Malaysia (Malaysian Qualifications Register, MQR)".
4. Write BM proper nouns for Malaysian bodies: Agensi Kelayakan Malaysia (MQA), Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi (KPT), Pembangunan Sumber Manusia Berhad (HRD Corp).
5. Use "tertakluk kepada", "disahkan secara bertulis", "diluluskan", "ditentukan oleh" when an outcome depends on a third party, and name that party (HRD Corp, CMI, ABC).
6. Write money as RM10,000.00 (no space, comma thousands, two decimals when the fact carries them); "RM17 juta" style for large rounded sums.
7. Write dates as "22–23 Ogos 2026", "9 pagi hingga 6 petang", "Sabtu dan Ahad"; BM month names always (Jan, Feb, Mac, Apr, Mei, Jun, Jul, Ogos, Sep, Okt, Nov, Dis).
8. Use numerals for programme facts in running text and headlines (6 bulan, 3 sesi, 12 modul), as news and universities do; words only for one-off small counts ("satu projek").
9. Use the word+numeral form in formal condition lines if the PDF or terms page imitates official style: "enam (6) bulan".
10. Start FAQ answers with the direct answer: "Boleh.", "Tidak.", "Bukan.", then the reason.
11. Use "-kan" verbs with their meN- prefix in prose: "akan mengesahkan", "menggunakan", "menempah".
12. Pair every long sentence with a short follow-up (Siti Khadijah rhythm) on hero, campaign and e-mail copy.
13. Use "Sebab itu", "Jadi", "Justeru", "Oleh itu" as connectives; the first two for warm pages, the last two for formal pages.
14. Close e-mails and warm pages with "Semoga…" or a plain invitation, not an exclamation.
15. Use "peserta" for the person in the programme, "pemohon" for the person before enrolment, "majikan" for the funding party.
16. Use CTA verbs that name the action and its purpose: "Dapatkan panduan program 2026", "Tempah sesi perbincangan program", "Hantar pertanyaan untuk syarikat anda", "Minta cadangan korporat".
17. Use "Salam sejahtera, {Nama}," as the e-mail salutation; "Tuan/Puan" when no name is known.
18. Keep sentence-case headlines; Title Case only for named documents and CTAs.
19. Render "endorsed" as "diperakukan" and "recognised" as "diiktiraf"; keep "diakreditasi" strictly for MQA accreditation.
20. Use "projek amali perniagaan" everywhere for the applied project.

**Don't**
21. Don't use "kamu", "awak", "korang", "-lah", "nak", "tak", "macam", "tapi" outside a quoted testimonial.
22. Don't drop the meN- prefix in body text ("akan sahkan", "boleh anda guna") — that is headline or speech grammar.
23. Don't translate or italicise the English credential names, and don't write "MBA Eksekutif" for this programme.
24. Don't use "anugerah" alone for a scholarship award; don't use "dalam bentuk bertulis".
25. Don't use "program awam" for the open-enrolment programme; use "program terbuka".
26. Don't use "laluan lanjutan", "peringkat seterusnya" or "fasa kedua" for Chartered Manager — governance treats it as a separate optional route.
27. Don't use "disokong" for "endorsed".
28. Don't use tabloid hooks: quoted-phrase headlines, "talian hayat", "impian", "gemilang", "berbaloi", "jatuh cinta", "Cepat mohon", ampersands in headings.
29. Don't use exclamation marks on fee, credential, FAQ or programme pages; at most one on a campaign page.
30. Don't write "RM 10,000", "RM10 000" or "10,000 ringgit".
31. Don't use English month abbreviations (Aug, Sep, Oct, Dec) in BM date strings.
32. Don't use "biasanya", "kebiasaannya", "selalunya", "sering", "kebanyakan", "lebih kurang", "kira-kira" before a fact — governance forbids qualifiers.
33. Don't use "Mohon Sekarang" for the enquiry form; an enquiry is not an application (governance).
34. Don't use "Muat turun" for a form that requests a guide by e-mail; use "Dapatkan".
35. Don't mix "atas talian" and "dalam talian"; use "dalam talian".
36. Don't use "emel", "email"; use "e-mel".
37. Don't use "fi" for fees; "yuran" for programme fees, "caj" only for a one-off charge.
38. Don't use "kekosongan kohort"; the seat is "tempat".
39. Don't put the name before the greeting ("Ali, salam sejahtera,").
40. Don't calque English idioms ("the guide will still be honest", "no time out of the business", "live calendar", "maps the steps"); translate the meaning.

## 6. Audit of the current site Malay — 15 priority items

Files: `src/lib/content-ms.ts` (C), `src/components/site/FutureCommerceHome.tsx` ms block (H), `src/app/(ms)/ms/page.tsx` (P), `src/app/(ms)/ms/fees/page.tsx` (F), `src/app/(ms)/ms/executive-mba/page.tsx` (E), `src/app/(ms)/ms/faq/page.tsx` (Q), `src/lib/nurture-email.ts` MS block (N), `src/lib/content.ts` CTA_LABELS.ms (L). Every correction keeps the facts (6 bulan, 6 hari latihan, 3 sesi, 12 modul, RM10,000.00, LIFE Innoversity, CMI, MQA, HRD Corp, ABC, Right Dots Resources) unchanged.

**1. "Pengurus Bekerja" as a title noun (P meta title; N guide name).**
- Current: "Executive MBA Enam Bulan untuk Pengurus Bekerja"; "Beberapa hari lalu anda memohon Panduan Pengurus Bekerja untuk Future Ready Executive MBA."
- Why: "pengurus bekerja" is a word-for-word calque of "working manager"; the university corpus expresses this with a relative clause or "pengalaman bekerja" (4.1 "enam (6) bulan pengalaman bekerja"; 4.5 "ramai profesional yang bekerja"). Headlines also prefer numerals (4.7 "18-36 bulan"; 1.7 "18 pelajar").
- Corrected: "Executive MBA 6 Bulan untuk Pengurus yang Sedang Bekerja"; "…anda meminta Panduan untuk Pengurus yang Bekerja bagi Future Ready Executive MBA." (Also "memohon" implies a formal application; "meminta" matches a guide request.)

**2. "perbualan program" CTA family (L, N, Q).**
- Current: "Aturkan perbualan program" (L); "Atur perbualan program" (N ctaLabel); "itulah sebabnya perbualan program wujud" (N); "ini bukan tawaran kemasukan atau komitmen bayaran" (C FAQ).
- Why: "perbualan program" is not a BM collocation; corpus CTAs are verb + concrete object ("Mohon Sekarang", "Muat Turun Risalah", "Hubungi Kami!", 4.5; "KETAHUI LEBIH LANJUT", 3.1). The site is also inconsistent (Aturkan/Atur).
- Corrected: CTA "Tempah sesi perbincangan program" everywhere; body "itulah gunanya sesi perbincangan program"; governance CTA "Discuss programme fit" → "Bincangkan kesesuaian program".

**3. CMI wording: "disokong" and an inconsistent programme name (H, N, Q).**
- Current: "Executive MBA dalam Future Ready Business Leadership, dianugerahkan dan disokong oleh CMI." (H); "Ini ialah Executive MBA dalam kepimpinan perniagaan masa hadapan, dianugerahkan dan disokong oleh CMI." (Q); "Dianugerahkan dan disokong" (H h3).
- Why: "disokong" means "supported/backed", not "endorsed"; official BM uses "diperakukan" for a formal endorsement and "diiktiraf" for recognition (3.9, 3.11, 4.1). The programme name is rendered three ways (English, half-translated, fully translated); news keeps English programme names verbatim with an acronym in brackets (1.9, 1.2) and governance requires certificate wording.
- Corrected: "Executive MBA on Future Ready Business Leadership, dianugerahkan dan diperakukan oleh CMI." in all three places; h3 "Dianugerahkan dan diperakukan"; FAQ: "Ini ialah program Executive MBA on Future Ready Business Leadership, dianugerahkan dan diperakukan oleh CMI. Program 6 bulan ini ialah program pembangunan profesional dan pendidikan eksekutif — bukan ijazah akademik yang diakreditasi MQA atau kelayakan yang dikawal selia."

**4. "laluan lanjutan Chartered Manager" (C programme intro; E).**
- Current: "Jika anda layak, anda boleh bertanya secara berasingan tentang laluan lanjutan Chartered Manager."
- Why: "lanjutan" means continuation/advanced stage, which is exactly the "fixed second programme stage" the governance file forbids; the site's own section heading already has the right term ("Laluan Chartered Manager yang berasingan").
- Corrected: "Jika anda layak, anda boleh bertanya secara berasingan tentang laluan pilihan Chartered Manager, yang ditentukan oleh CMI."

**5. "Sebarang anugerah … dalam bentuk bertulis" (C fees intro, fees section, comparison row).**
- Current: "Sebarang anugerah dan yuran peserta berkenaan disahkan secara individu dalam bentuk bertulis; biasiswa tidak automatik dan bukan kod diskaun."
- Why: "anugerah" as a bare noun reads as a prize (1.7 "Anugerah Pelajar Terbaik"); the verb "dianugerahkan biasiswa" is fine (1.9) but the noun needs "biasiswa". "dalam bentuk bertulis" is a calque; official BM says "secara bertulis" / "kebenaran bertulis" (3.9). "tidak automatik" needs a verb.
- Corrected: "Sebarang pemberian biasiswa dan yuran peserta yang berkenaan disahkan secara individu dan secara bertulis; biasiswa tidak diberikan secara automatik dan bukan kod diskaun."

**6. "boleh dinilai untuk biasiswa" (F meta description; C fees).**
- Current: "Pemohon Malaysia yang layak boleh dinilai untuk biasiswa terhad dan terpilih; anugerah serta yuran peserta disahkan secara bertulis."
- Why: "dinilai untuk" is a calque of "considered for"; KPT writes "boleh dipertimbangkan untuk" (3.14). "terhad dan terpilih" stacks two adjectives on "biasiswa" where the corpus attaches the manner to the award ("dianugerahkan secara terpilih").
- Corrected: "Yuran standard RM10,000.00. Pemohon Malaysia yang layak boleh dipertimbangkan untuk biasiswa terhad yang dianugerahkan secara terpilih; pemberian biasiswa dan yuran peserta disahkan secara bertulis."

**7. "Ubah kerumitan ke keputusan" (C method title).**
- Current: "Ubah kerumitan ke keputusan"
- Why: "ubah … ke" takes a place, not a result; BM uses "menjadi" or "kepada" for transformation — the same file already writes "mengubah keputusan menjadi pelan tindakan".
- Corrected: "Ubah kerumitan menjadi keputusan"

**8. "akan sahkan kekosongan kohort" (C intakes intro).**
- Current: "Pasukan program akan sahkan kekosongan kohort apabila membalas pertanyaan anda."
- Why: prefix-dropping ("akan sahkan") is headline/speech grammar (1.1 "taja", 1.9 "rekod"); body prose in every formal source keeps the meN- prefix ("akan menawarkan", 2.6; "akan diproses", 3.14). "kekosongan kohort" is not a collocation; a seat is "tempat".
- Corrected: "Pasukan program akan mengesahkan kekosongan tempat dalam kohort apabila membalas pertanyaan anda."

**9. Home fragments and "sesi" vs "kohort" (H).**
- Current: "Kejelasan program dan kohort." (mediaTitle); "Pilih sesi yang bersesuaian dengan anda." (intakesTitle) — while the block lists cohorts and C says "Pilih kohort yang paling sesuai dengan jadual kerja anda."
- Why: "Kejelasan program dan kohort" is a noun-for-noun calque of "Programme and cohort clarity"; BM headlines carry a verb or a full noun phrase with an adjective (4.1 "Maklumat Penting Yang Anda Perlu Tahu…"). "sesi" here contradicts the site's own term "kohort".
- Corrected: "Maklumat program dan kohort yang jelas." ; "Pilih kohort yang sesuai dengan jadual anda."

**10. E-mail salutation order and fallback (N greeting; nurture-email.ts fallback "anda").**
- Current: `${name}, salam sejahtera,` → renders as "Ali, salam sejahtera," or, with no name, "anda, salam sejahtera,".
- Why: BM greetings put the salutation first ("Salam sejahtera, Encik Ali,"); "anda" cannot be a vocative. Formal letters use "Tuan/Puan" when the name is unknown.
- Corrected: `Salam sejahtera, ${name},` with fallback name "Tuan/Puan" (not "anda"); sign-off "Salam hormat, Pasukan Program Future Ready Executive MBA".

**11. "projek aplikasi" (N, two places).**
- Current: "projek aplikasi anda dijalankan dalam perniagaan anda sendiri di antara sesi"; "Bimbingan bersama projek aplikasi ke atas perniagaan anda sendiri".
- Why: "aplikasi" means a software application; the site's own pages use "projek amali" (C comparison) and "projek perniagaan"; the governance term is "applied business project".
- Corrected: "projek amali perniagaan anda dijalankan dalam perniagaan anda sendiri di antara sesi"; "Bimbingan bersama projek amali perniagaan ke atas perniagaan anda sendiri".

**12. Calendar sentence in nurture step 2 (N).**
- Current: "Kalendar langsung bagi kohort 2026 yang masih tinggal, dalam Bahasa Inggeris dan Mandarin, terdapat di laman web. Jika satu-satu tarikh hampir sesuai, maklumkan kepada pasukan sebelum anda menolaknya — perbualan mengenai kalendar biasanya yang paling ringkas."
- Why: "kalendar langsung" (live calendar) and "yang masih tinggal" (remaining) are calques — "masih tinggal" reads as "still living"; "satu-satu tarikh" is not idiomatic; "biasanya" is a governance-forbidden qualifier. Universities say "Sesi Pengambilan" and "tarikh" (4.6).
- Corrected: "Jadual terkini kohort 2026 yang masih dibuka, dalam Bahasa Inggeris dan Mandarin, boleh dilihat di laman web. Jika ada tarikh yang hampir sesuai, maklumkan kepada pasukan program sebelum anda menolaknya — soal jadual boleh diselesaikan dalam satu perbualan ringkas."

**13. Idiom calques in nurture step 3 and format line (N).**
- Current: "Jika masa ini kurang sesuai, itu satu keputusan yang sah — panduan itu akan tetap jujur pada kohort akan datang."; format value "…tanpa perlu keluar dari kerja".
- Why: "sah" means legally valid; "jujur" (honest) cannot be predicated of a guide in BM; "keluar dari kerja" reads as resigning. Governance asks for meaning, not idiom. University copy expresses the same idea with "sambil mengimbangi tanggungjawab kerja" (4.5).
- Corrected: "Jika masa ini kurang sesuai, itu keputusan yang wajar — kandungan panduan itu tetap sama untuk kohort akan datang."; "Tempoh 6 bulan · 3 hujung minggu berjadual sepanjang program · anda terus bekerja sepenuh masa".

**14. "Program awam Malaysia" and "eksekutif dan ke atas" (C FAQ).**
- Current: "Program awam Malaysia berjalan selama 6 bulan dengan 6 hari latihan yang ditetapkan."; "Program ini terbuka kepada eksekutif dan ke atas, termasuk pengurus kanan, pengarah, pemilik perniagaan dan pengasas."
- Why: "program awam" reads as a government programme (cf. "universiti awam", "Perkhidmatan awam", 1.7, 1.10); the open-enrolment sense is "program terbuka". "eksekutif dan ke atas" is a calque of "executives and above"; BM ranks with "peringkat … ke atas" (4.8 "hingga ke peringkat pengurusan kanan").
- Corrected: "Program terbuka di Malaysia berjalan selama 6 bulan dengan 6 hari latihan yang ditetapkan."; "Program ini terbuka kepada peserta peringkat eksekutif ke atas, termasuk pengurus kanan, pengarah, pemilik perniagaan dan pengasas."

**15. Date and numeral rendering on BM pages (C intakes; C fees; C fees title).**
- Current: intake rows render `${intake.s1}` etc. from `content.ts`, producing "Sesi pertama: 22–23 Aug; sesi kedua: 19–20 Sep; sesi ketiga: 17–18 Oct." on the BM page; fees: "ansuran kad kredit RHB selama 6–12 bulan dan pelan bayaran empat bulan oleh ABC"; H1: "Yuran standard RM10,000.00; biasiswa terhad, dinilai berdasarkan kelayakan".
- Why: BM prose never uses English month abbreviations (1.9 "26 Ogos 2026", 2.8 "29 Februari hingga 29 Mac 2024"); "Aug/Oct/Dec" are wrong in BM ("Ogos/Okt/Dis"). Mixing "6–12" with "empat" in one sentence breaks the corpus habit of numerals for durations (4.7 "18-36 bulan"). A semicolon in an H1 does not occur in any corpus headline; colons, commas and dashes do.
- Corrected: map months in the BM renderer (Jan, Feb, Mac, Apr, Mei, Jun, Jul, Ogos, Sep, Okt, Nov, Dis) so the row reads "Sesi pertama: 22–23 Ogos; sesi kedua: 19–20 Sep; sesi ketiga: 17–18 Okt. Sabtu dan Ahad, 9 pagi hingga 6 petang."; "Pilihan termasuk ansuran kad kredit RHB selama 6–12 bulan dan pelan bayaran 4 bulan daripada ABC."; H1 "Yuran standard RM10,000.00 — biasiswa terhad, tertakluk kepada penilaian kelayakan".

## 7. Self-validation checklist for a rewriting agent

Run each check against the whole BM output before returning it.

1. Search for `kamu|awak|korang|engkau|nak |tak |macam |-lah\b` — expect zero hits outside quoted testimonials.
2. Search for `akan [a-z]+kan\b` where the verb lacks meN- (e.g. "akan sahkan", "boleh guna") — rewrite with the prefix.
3. Search for `Aug|Sep\b|Oct|Dec|Jan\b|Feb\b|Mar\b|May\b|Jul\b` in BM date strings — replace with BM month names; verify "Sep" is intended BM.
4. Search for `RM \d|RM\d{1,3}\d{3}\b(?!,)` — every amount must be "RM10,000.00" style with no space and comma thousands.
5. Search for `biasanya|kebiasaannya|selalunya|sering|kebanyakan|lebih kurang|kira-kira|hampir semua` — remove or replace with an exact fact.
6. Search for `perbualan program|Pengurus Bekerja|projek aplikasi|laluan lanjutan|program awam|dan ke atas|dalam bentuk bertulis|dinilai untuk|disokong oleh CMI|atas talian|emel\b|kepemimpinan` — expect zero hits.
7. Confirm every occurrence of these identifiers is verbatim and untranslated: `HRD Corp Claimable Course`, `Allowable Cost Matrix`, `e-TRiS`, `CMI Certificate of Recognition`, `Chartered Manager`, `Foundation Chartered Manager (fCMgr)`, `Executive MBA on Future Ready Business Leadership`, `LIFE Innoversity`, `Agensi Kelayakan Malaysia (MQA)`.
8. Confirm every fact value matches `content.ts`: 6 bulan, 6 hari latihan, 3 sesi, 12 modul, RM10,000.00, 17 kohort, 154 graduan, cohort dates and days; no new numbers introduced.
9. Confirm each fee/scholarship/HRD Corp/CMI sentence names the decision-maker (HRD Corp, CMI, ABC, pasukan program) and uses "tertakluk kepada" or "disahkan secara bertulis" where the outcome is conditional.
10. Confirm Chartered Manager is described only as "laluan pilihan CMI yang berasingan" with "kelayakan, penilaian, keahlian dan yuran ditentukan oleh CMI".
11. Confirm no sentence promises promotion, salary, ROI or "berjaya" outcomes; capability verbs only (membentangkan, menggunakan, menilai).
12. Confirm every CTA is verb + object (+ purpose) and none uses "Mohon Sekarang" for the enquiry form.
13. Confirm e-mail salutations read "Salam sejahtera, {Nama}," with fallback "Tuan/Puan", and sign-offs name "Pasukan Program".
14. Count exclamation marks: zero on programme, fees, FAQ and credential pages; at most one per campaign page or e-mail.
15. Read every H1/H2 aloud: each must be a full noun phrase or a clause with a verb, sentence-case, no semicolons.
16. Check that FAQ answers open with the direct answer word ("Boleh.", "Tidak.", "Bukan.").
17. Check that "anda" does not appear inside a fact sentence about fees, levy, accreditation or refunds.
18. Check pronoun consistency: "kami"/"pasukan program" for the team, "peserta" inside the programme, "pemohon" before enrolment, "majikan" for funding.
19. Spot-check five random sentences for length: hero/e-mail ≤ 20 words, programme/FAQ ≤ 25, fees ≤ 25 with one condition per sentence.
20. Confirm the EN and BM versions of every fact sentence say the same thing (governance equivalence rule), then confirm the BM sentence would not read as a translation to a KL executive — the test is whether it could appear unchanged on a UM/UNITAR programme page.
