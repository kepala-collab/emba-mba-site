# Owner vocabulary rules (set by the site owner on 2026-09-04)

These override the register guides where they differ.

## Rendering the owner's messaging vocabulary in Malay and Chinese

- Local term first, English in round brackets on first use on a page, then the local term alone. Owner's example: **kepimpinan berkhidmat (servant leadership)**.
- Malay renderings to use:
  - servant leadership: kepimpinan berkhidmat (servant leadership)
  - progress: kemajuan (progress)
  - hope: harapan (hope), never tied to a career or salary outcome
  - change of experience: perubahan pengalaman is a calque; express it as what the reader can do or see differently, for example "cara anda membuat keputusan berubah"
  - the right thing, not just the good thing: perkara yang betul, bukan sekadar perkara yang baik
  - the right job to be done: tugas sebenar yang perlu diselesaikan (the right job to be done); never "kerja yang betul"
  - the five S: keep the English labels once, glossed as Kelangsungan, Keselamatan, Sosial, Kejayaan, Khidmat; "Kejayaan" is allowed only as that capitalised label
- Chinese renderings to use (same pattern: Chinese term, English in brackets on first use):
  - servant leadership: 仆人式领导 (servant leadership)
  - progress: 进步 (progress); hope: 希望 (hope), never tied to a promised outcome
  - change of experience: express as a change in how the reader decides and leads, not as a literal 体验改变
  - the right thing vs the good thing: 对的事，而不只是好的事
  - the right job to be done: 真正要完成的任务 (the right job to be done)
  - the five S: keep the English labels once, glossed as 生存、安全、社交、成功、服务

## Governance interactions

- "Deserving comes out of serving" and "he is greatest who serves most" are posture, never a claim about the scholarship, fees or HRD Corp, and never a promise. The audit's own banned-word list means "most" cannot appear in that sentence on the site; render it as leadership measured by the people it serves.
- Facts never change and are always the constants in `src/lib/content.ts`, `content-ms.ts`, `content-zh.ts`.
