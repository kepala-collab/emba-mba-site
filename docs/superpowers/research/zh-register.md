# Malaysian Simplified Chinese (zh-Hans-MY) register guide for futurereadymba.com/zh

Research date: 3 September 2026. Written for the rewriting agent and the content owner.
Scope: register, vocabulary and typography only. Nothing in this guide changes a programme fact; every
correction in section 6 preserves the facts and controlled terminology in `SEO-CONTENT-GOVERNANCE.md`.

Owner's brief: the Chinese copy should read like the editorial, decorative style of the Malaysian Chinese
dailies' education sections (星洲日报 新教育 / 学海, 南洋商报, 中国报, 东方日报, 光华日报), not like
flat translated marketing. This guide records what that style actually looks like on the page, where the
press style must NOT be copied because governance forbids it (urgency devices, hedged numbers, vague
"资格认证" claims), and how to apply the result page by page.

Quote rule used throughout: each quotation from a source is at most 15 words / a short Chinese clause,
followed by the URL or the source number from section 1.

## 2. Register findings by group, with evidence

### 2.1 Group 1 — Sin Chew Daily (新教育 / 学海 / 市场情报 / 星洲商学院)

**Headline conventions**
- Two-part headlines split by a plain space, no end punctuation, each half a balanced verb phrase: "高管也要进修迎新挑战 从AI到领导力更新商业思维" (S1). Feature headlines may use a comma and a 四字词 as the second half: "网络之便，在职进修乘势而起" (S2).
- Section/category tags sit in 【】 in front of the headline on 学海: "【校园报道】获选AFS交换生计划 维中生赴日交流4个月" (S7). Numbers in headlines are Arabic (4个月, 139清寒生).
- Colloquial or career pieces allow one ！: "这两道题才是关键！" (S5). The serious 新教育 feature (S1) has no ！ anywhere.
- Rhetorical-question headlines are common in 新教育 features: "读研，是人云亦云还是真的适合自己？" (S3).
- Sub-site advertorials add "——" for a subtitle: "……专业硕士课程 —— 一趟充满活力的学习体验" (S13).

**Body rhythm**
- Ledes open with a situational clause + a 四字词, then a plain statement: "商业环境瞬息万变……也不能停止学习" (S1); "在目前疫情未退、百业萧条的情况下" (S2). Typical sentence 20–35 characters; a 12–18 character sentence closes each paragraph.
- Decoration is carried by one or two 四字词 per paragraph (瞬息万变, 身居高位, 场场爆满, 趋之若鹜, 出身不凡 in S1; 半途而废, 无所适从 in S2). Beyond two per paragraph the text tips into advertorial (compare S13: 脱颖而出, 日新月异, 孜孜不倦, 更上一层楼, 一应俱全, 数一数二 in three paragraphs — this is the ceiling the owner's "decorative" brief should NOT exceed).
- Facts are attributed to a named person with title before the claim: "亚洲商学院（ASB）高管教育中心执行总监黄素玲透露" (S1). Quoted speech uses "" and sits inside the reporting sentence.
- Case-story features use a person as the through-line: "从事人力资源工作的辛美玲……报读开放大学……" (S2); first person in 大都会: "一边工作一边进修的生活很忙碌" (S4).
- 排比 of three appears in reasons/benefit lists, often as a numbered list (S3: "1.我想我还没有准备好就业 2.……").

**Vocabulary (Malaysian forms observed)**
- Executives: 高管, 企业高管, 高阶主管, 企业最高领导层及董事会成员 (S1); managers: 管理人员, 主管 (S1); CEO is 首席执行员 (S5) — not mainland 首席执行官.
- Working people: 在职人士 (S10 headline), 上班族 (S2 "为忙碌的上班族打开了方便之门"), 打工族 (S10), 忙碌人士 (S10).
- Further study: 进修 (in-work study, S1/S2/S10), 深造 (going away to study, S8 "到英国飞行学院深造"), 报读 (to enrol on, S2/S3), 半工读 (S10), 获颁学位 (S2).
- MBA/EMBA kept Latin: "提供 MBA、EMBA、高管教育等课程" (S1); 高管教育 = executive education; 高管进修课程 (S1).
- Institutions: 大学, 学院, 大专院校, 学府; 开放大学 (S2).
- Ringgit: 令吉 in prose; "早鸟优惠1600令吉（含8%SST）" (S12); "耗资了500万令吉" (S10).
- HRD Corp: "人力资源发展机构（HRD Corp）注册雇主" and "人力资源发展税（HRD levy）" (S11); "可申领大马人力资源发展机构员工补助（HRDF）" (S12); grant = 培训津贴 (S11). Account = 户口 ("仅保留户口里的1万令吉", S11) — Malaysian, not mainland 账户.
- Scholarship family: 奖学金 (S6), 辅助金/奖学金 (S9); applicant = 申请者 (S9).

**Address and CTA**
- News features do not address the reader. Advertorials switch to 你: "身为企业主的你，是否知道……" (S11); "为你的企业『驾驭AI』" (S12). 您 does not appear in the press samples; it belongs to institutions and corporates (see 2.5, 2.6).
- Course sales page CTAs (S12): "现在就即刻报名，万勿错失良机！" / "名额有限，先到先得！" — this is the local commercial idiom, but it is exactly the urgency device `SEO-CONTENT-GOVERNANCE.md` forbids. Do not copy.
- Softer advertorial close (S11): "现在正是培训与提升团队的好机会！" Still an urgency device; do not copy.
- Notice-style close (S13): label lines "日期：… 时间：… 地点：… 报名：… 更多详情：…" — this label-block form is reusable.

**Typography**
- Full-width ，。：；（）、 throughout. Straight/curly double quotes "" for speech and scare-quotes on the main site (S1: "因为高管想知道……"); 『』 used for inner emphasis on S12; 「」 seen on the older 学海 sub-site (S8). No consistent 「」 house style.
- No space between Chinese and Latin/numerals in press body text: "2026年高管教育排名", "位居第94名" (S1), "1600令吉（含8%SST）" (S12).
- Arabic numerals for counts, dates, money; 万 for ten-thousands: "500万令吉" (S10), "1万令吉" (S11).
- Times in Malaysian English style inside Chinese on advertorials: "9.30am – 12.30pm" (S13); press proper uses "上午9时至下午5时" (S12).
- Older CMS pages carry half-width commas in Chinese text ("在这个随便丢一颗石头就能砸中大学生的年代,"), an artefact, not a convention (S3).

**Fees, scholarships, eligibility, accreditation — how it is hedged**
- Official-sounding eligibility constructions: "申请者必须是……的马来西亚公民" ; "评审团选出的结果是最后的决定" ; "恕不处理" ; "有权增删以上的规则" (S9).
- Fee statements are exact and attributed: "早鸟优惠1600令吉（含8%SST）/ 标准收费1750令吉" (S12).
- Grant statements name the body, the mechanism and the condition: "凡是人力资源发展机构（HRD Corp）注册雇主……" (S11).
- Recognition claims in advertorials stay vague ("国际认证文凭") — the site must be more precise than the press here.

### 2.2 Group 2 — Nanyang Siang Pau

**Headlines**: tag + two parts: "【视频】推出专业与硕士博士课 南方大学学院设商学院" (N1); business exclusives use "【独家】" (N3). Columns append the author after a slash: "谁是最佳主管人选?/陈绣茗" (N2).

**Body**: dateline in brackets opens news: "（新山21日讯）" (N1). Business register is brisker than 新教育: "老板们注意，……其15%余额就要"飞走"了！" (N3). Sub-heads use three-beat 排比: "欠实用 缺人手 怕跳槽" (N3). Institutional quotes are long and formal: "更加偏重以工商界实际需求为基础的培训" (N1). Advertorial ceiling (N5): "走出去，学回来"; "教育的本质是唤醒、连接与行动" — rhythmic but content-thin; do not imitate density.

**Vocabulary**: EMBA glossed once then Latin: "中文高级工商管理硕士（EMBA）课程" (N4); "MBA课程" stays Latin (N4). Employers = 雇主, 企业, 业者, 老板 (N3). HRD Corp = "人力资源发展机构（HRDC）" and "人力资源发展基金（HRDF）补助金"; levy paid = 缴纳款 / 缴纳额 (N3). MQA = "马来西亚学术资格鉴定机构（MQA）的认证" (N1). Scholarship = 研究生奖学金; fee support = "75%的学费赞助" (N1). Managers in columns: 主管, 老板, 员工, 业主 (N2). Entrepreneurs: 企业家, 企业创办人 (N5).

**Hedging**: "当局不接受上诉" ; "雇主须在6月14日前参与……" (N3) — 须 + deadline is the standard official construction.

### 2.3 Group 3 — China Press and Oriental Daily

**Headlines**: colon attribution + three short beats: "王培荣：拿全额奖学金 成绩中等 也有机会" (C1). Oriental uses a full-width space 　 between headline halves: "圆梦升学、投身教育　董总贷助学金5月22日截止" (O2); "【教育】改变思维占先机　UCSI蓝海政策MBA" (O5). China Press marks notices with ◤◢: "◤院校动态◢……在职专班等你报读" (C4). 对仗 headline on an institution feature: "方正做人 仪礼待人 方仪学院实战教育 终身学习" (C5).

**Body rhythm**: fact-dense reporting with exact figures: "首次付款1050令吉，其中50令吉为行政费" (O1); "学费为2万令吉起" (O3); "每年贷款额介于8000令吉至1万令吉" (O2). Decorative openers only on features: "方正做人皆教育，仪礼待人皆实践。" (C5). Quoted academics carry the argument: "晋升管理阶层的踏脚石" ; "僧多粥少" (O5). Advertorial (O4) stacks milestones: "迈向人生与事业的新里程碑" ; "意义非凡" — readable, but its credential wording is loose: "获得执行工商管理硕士（eMBA）资格认证" (O4). The site must not borrow "资格认证" for the CMI Certificate of Recognition.

**Vocabulary**: MBA glossed then Latin: "工商管理硕士（MBA）是各大专院校都会提供的基本进修科目" (O5); EMBA as "执行工商管理硕士课程（eMBA）" (O4). Executives: 企业领袖, 管理人才, 企业高层管理人员, 专业人士 (O4). In-work class: 在职专班 (C4). Intake/admission verbs: 开班, 开放招生, 备有全职与兼职模式, 全年开放报名与申请, 皆可报读 (O3). Pathway = 管道 ("升学管道", C1) — not mainland 渠道. Facebook = 面子书 (C5). Loans vs bursaries vs scholarships kept distinct: 贷学金, 助学金, 奖学金, 贷助学金 (O2). Instalment = 学费分期付款 (O1). Enquiry = 咨询 / 谘询 (O2 uses the variant 谘询时间).

**Hedging / official**: "鼓励符合资格者把握机会，踊跃提出申请" ; "如有任何疑问，请联系……" (O2); "并获得校评议会（Senate）批准者皆可报读" (O3). Note the press itself hedges numbers ("学费调整约3%", O1) — governance forbids 约 before a number on this site.

### 2.7 What separates the groups, and what makes the newspaper education-section style "editorial"

1. **Where decoration sits.** Newspapers put 四字词 and 对仗 in the headline and the first two sentences, then go fact-dense; institutions put a little decoration in "about us" and none in fees/admission; corporates put it in slogans and nowhere else. Advertorials spread it everywhere (S13, N5, O4), which is why they read as advertising.
2. **Attribution.** Editorial prose attributes every judgement to a named person with a title ("执行总监黄素玲透露", S1; "校长蔡润煌教授解释", N1). Marketing asserts in the institution's own voice. Attribution is the single strongest "editorial" signal available to this site (ABC, the programme director, named faculty).
3. **Numbers first, adjectives second.** Press ledes carry the number before any adjective: "拨出50万令吉给研究生奖学金" (N1); "学费为2万令吉起" (O3). Translated marketing leads with adjectives.
4. **Rhythm.** Press paragraphs are 2–4 sentences, mixing a 25–35 character sentence with a short closer; institutions write list items; corporates write one-sentence paragraphs.
5. **The reader.** Press features never say 您; they say 在职人士 / 上班族 / 高管 as a group and let the reader self-select. Institutions say 学生 / 申请者. Corporates say 您. The site's pages are institutional, so 您 stays; but headlines and article ledes should adopt the press habit of naming the group rather than the pronoun.
6. **Malaysian lexis is shared across all six groups** (令吉, 大专, 报读, 进修, 华语, 首席执行员, 面子书, 户口, 管道, 数码, 行销) — it is not a newspaper mannerism, it is the local language. Mainland lexis (渠道, 首席执行官, 创始人, 账户, 营销, 套餐, 高考, 本科) reads as foreign in every group.
7. **The one press habit the site must refuse**: commercial urgency ("名额有限，先到先得！", S12) and hedged numbers ("约3%", O1). Governance overrides the corpus here.

## 5. Do / Don't

### Register and structure
1. **Do** put the decoration where the press puts it: one 四字词 or a 对仗 pair in H1/H2 and in the first sentence; then facts. **Don't** exceed two 四字词 per paragraph in body copy (S13 and N5 show the advertorial ceiling).
2. **Do** open programme and insight ledes with number-first facts ("6 个月、6 个培训日、3 个指定周末"). **Don't** open with adjectives ("卓越的", "顶尖的", "全方位的").
3. **Do** attribute judgements to a named person with a title (ABC, the programme lead, a named faculty member), as S1 and N1 do. **Don't** assert quality in the site's own voice.
4. **Do** use three-beat 排比 for benefit lists ("欠实用 缺人手 怕跳槽" pattern, N3). **Don't** run 排比 longer than three beats.
5. **Do** name the group in headlines (在职管理者, 企业主, 高管). **Don't** put 您/你 in a headline.
6. **Do** keep 您 for institutional pages, emails and PDFs (Maxis, Public Bank, current site). **Don't** switch to 你 except inside quoted speech in insight articles; never mix on one page.
7. **Do** use topic-first, active Chinese: "申请由雇主提出". **Don't** start sentences with 它 / 这 as a stand-in for an abstract English subject ("它不包含在…").
8. **Do** close every fee, scholarship, refund, HRD Corp and CMI paragraph with the controlling document or decision-maker (以书面为准 / 由 CMI 决定 / 依据 Allowable Cost Matrix). **Don't** leave a condition implied.
9. **Do** borrow the institutions' procedural verbs for actions: 报读, 索取, 预约, 查看, 咨询. **Don't** use app-store verbs 获取 / 下载 / 领取 / 点击这里 for CTAs.
10. **Don't** copy the newspapers' commercial urgency: 名额有限、先到先得、万勿错失良机、立即报名、现在就 (S12, S11). Governance forbids urgency devices; the CTA must name action and purpose.
11. **Don't** hedge numbers the way the press does ("约3%", O1): no 约 / 大约 / 通常 / 一般 / 左右 before a quantity.
12. **Don't** borrow the private providers' loose credential language ("资格认证", "国际认证文凭"). Use the controlled forms in the glossary.
13. **Don't** call ABC or Right Dots Resources a 学院 / 大学 / 学府, and don't call the programme a 学位课程.
14. **Do** keep the programme name, CMI terms, Chartered Manager, Allowable Cost Matrix, e-TRiS and cohort IDs in Latin; gloss once per page in full-width brackets.

### Vocabulary

18. **Do** use 届 / 班 for cohorts. **Don't** use 班次 (transport schedule) or 批次.
19. **Do** use 线上说明会, 面谈, 预约通话. **Don't** use 在线, 视频会议, 线下.
20. **Do** distinguish 奖学金 / 助学金 / 贷学金 / 学费减免. **Don't** call the LIFE Innoversity award a 折扣 / 优惠 / 折扣码.

### Typography
21. Full-width punctuation only in Chinese text: ，。：；？！（）、—— . No half-width commas or periods inside Chinese sentences (S3's artefact).
22. Quotation marks: "" (U+201C/U+201D) for speech and scare-quotes, matching Sin Chew's main site. Do not introduce 「」; 『』 only for a term inside a quotation.
23. Brackets: full-width （） when the bracket sits in Chinese text, even if the content is Latin: 人力资源发展机构（HRD Corp）. Half-width () only inside an all-Latin string.
24. Numerals: Arabic for counts, dates, money, percentages, module codes; 万 for ten-thousands in prose (52万, 1万令吉) but keep the exact string RM10,000.00 where governance publishes it. Dates as 2026年9月4日至5日 (or with the site's spacing convention, below). Times as 上午 9 时至下午 6 时 (press: 上午9时至下午6时). Ranges with 至 in prose, en dash (–) in tables.
25. Spacing: the press does not space Chinese from Latin/numerals ("2026年", "1600令吉"); Maxis, New Era and the current site do ("6 个月", "RM 24,000"). Keep the site's existing pangu spacing, apply it consistently (including inside emails and JSON-LD text), and never insert spaces inside an official string (RM10,000.00, e-TRiS, fCMgr).
26. Dashes: —— (two em dashes) for a break or subtitle; · (middle dot) for eyebrow separators; no hyphen-minus in Chinese sentences.
27. Headlines: no trailing full stop; two beats separated by a space (press) or 　 (Oriental); colon only for attribution ("王培荣：…"); ！ never on programme, fee or recognition pages.
28. Never let a traditional glyph into simplified copy (豐, 優, 著 as in B1/I8/O4).
29. Simplified characters throughout (zh-Hans-MY); the variant 谘询 (O2) is acceptable in press but the site should standardise on 咨询.
