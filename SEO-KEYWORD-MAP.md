# SEO keyword and intent map

Reviewed 21 August 2026. This map adapts the supplied professional-learning seed research to the Malaysian market and the programme's verified public facts. It does not claim search volume.

## Rules

- One primary intent per canonical URL; use semantic variants in body copy instead of creating thin pages.
- Never describe the programme as an academic degree, regulated qualification, Level 7 programme, postgraduate qualification or automatic Chartered Manager route.
- Use `executive education`, `professional development programme` and `management development` to clarify programme category—not to imply an academic award.
- Describe potential career or business value as capabilities and workplace application. Do not guarantee promotion, salary, employment or commercial outcomes.
- Validate demand with Search Console query data before adding further landing pages.

## Deliberately excluded terms

The supplied research also contains broad or incompatible seeds. Do not target these unless the public offer changes and evidence is approved:

- `accredited Executive MBA`, `postgraduate certificate`, `executive master's degree`, `Level 7`, `academic credit`
- `self-paced`, `asynchronous`, `on-demand` or `100% online` where the actual delivery is live and cohort-based
- `guaranteed promotion`, `salary increase`, `job-ready certification` or guaranteed business ROI
- `automatic scholarship`, `100% HRD Corp approval` or automatic Chartered Manager status
- audience or industry pages that are not supported by a distinct programme proposition

## Measurement

Review Search Console every month for impressions, clicks, position and conversion by landing page. Consolidate pages when two URLs begin ranking for the same primary query. Create a new page only when query data shows a distinct user decision that the current canonical page cannot answer fully.
